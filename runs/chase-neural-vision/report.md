# Camera motion and relative-distance input pilot

Run: `FLY_DATA="$PWD/data/flybrain" .venv/bin/fly-fpv chase-vision --output runs/chase-neural-vision` after installing `.[fpv,perception]`. The [complete receipt](results.json) contains all camera observations, cell-drive values, neural commands, motor thrusts, and trajectories. Each condition uses the same three starting seeds, moving cow path, FPV camera, RAFT Small and Depth Anything V2 Small inference, frozen MaleCNS graph, fitted `DNa02` readout, image-size speed rule, and conventional quad stabilizer. The neural readout was fitted on bearing-only synthetic samples, then **locked** for all three conditions.

| Neural input | Follow-band fractions on three 18 s starts | Tracked | Mean decision time |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Tag bearing → `LC10a` | 0.646, 0.809, 0.789 | 2/3 | 183 ms |
| Bearing + optical flow → `T4/T5` | 0.586, 0.811, 0.806 | 2/3 | 188 ms |
| Flow + relative-distance cues → `LC15/LPLC2` | 0.691, 0.811, 0.806 | 2/3 | 185 ms |

The follow criterion is at least 70% of time in the 1.4–3.5 m behind-target band after the first 4 s. All nine neural flights completed, and no decision exceeded 400 ms. The geometric controller tracked in its one run. On **exactly the same 135 camera observations** from the bearing-only flights, flow changed 115 steering-command values and flow plus distance cues changed 116. Mean absolute changes were 0.074 and 0.080 on a command scale of −1 to +1, but turn *sign* changed only 5 and 4 times. The bearing-only readout chose the measured bearing sign on 116/118 nonneutral observations; flow scored 117/118 and flow plus distance 116/118. The open-loop bearing replay reproduced every original command exactly.

The [bearing video](bearing_demo.mp4), [flow video](flow_demo.mp4), and [flow plus distance video](flow_depth_demo.mp4) show the same four panels; the locally generated `flow_depth_2_maps.npz` contains the exact FPV decision frames and network outputs for the displayed depth run. This large array is excluded from Git and can be regenerated with the report command. `visual_input` in each JSON decision records which neural pools received stimulation. The first frame has no flow. All conditions spend the same compute budget on depth and flow inference, including bearing-only control where the outputs are withheld from neurons.

**Interpretation.** This establishes that the added visual channels reach the simulated cells and change the steering readout. It does not establish better pursuit: the success count stayed 2/3 and the paired follow fractions moved in both directions. The stimulation is an engineered proxy: up to 64 arbitrarily selected cells per T4/T5 subtype and side receive coarse directional flow; `LC15` receives a near/far flow-speed difference derived from learned relative depth; `LPLC2` receives depth-weighted expansion. These do not reconstruct retinal sampling, measured cell tuning, or a biological depth map. The [motion-circuit study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12488493/) supports T4/T5 flow processing; the [distance study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12652897/) implicates T4/T5 and LC15 in parallax-based relative distance; the [looming study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7457385/) identifies LPLC2 as an expansion detector. Our distance and looming encoders still need neural-response calibration.
